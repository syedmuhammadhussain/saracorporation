Bottom Wear Unit

A Dedicated Unit, Because Bottoms Are Different

Sara Corporation has assigned a separate unit to the production of bottom wear. Denim jeans, trousers and other bottoms are manufactured here for men, women and children.

Bottoms need machinery a shirt line simply does not carry — pocket welting, button holing, loop making and loop attachment. Keeping the unit separate means that equipment is always available and always set up, instead of being shared and reset between categories.
