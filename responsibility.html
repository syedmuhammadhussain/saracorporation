Responsibility

Sustainability, people and security are not a page in a policy folder here. They are how the floor runs every day.

Responsible by Design

Doing It Right, on Every Axis

Environment

Solar Power & Less Waste

A growing rooftop solar installation cuts our reliance on the grid, while automated cutting and planning reduce fabric waste and energy use. GRS certified for recycled materials.

Social

450+ People, Treated Properly

Fair wages, fair hours, a safe and clean workplace, ongoing training and real work life balance — verified through WRAP, SMETA and WCA audits.

Governance

Watched, Recorded, Protected

CCTV from gate to dispatch, a manned control room around the clock, controlled access and supervised loading supporting C-TPAT and GSV supply chain security.

Sustainability

Beyond Profit, Into Practice

Responsible business goes beyond profits. Sustainability is part of how we plan, produce and grow.

Rooftop solar power

A growing installation that steadily cuts our reliance on the grid.

Recycled materials

GRS certified use of recycled and responsible inputs.

Less waste

Automated cutting and marker planning reduce fabric waste on every order.

Efficient systems

Energy efficient machinery and continuous improvement of the process.

Our People

The Reason the Quality Holds

Machines shape the product, but people shape the company. The men and women of Sara Corporation bring skill, pride and years of experience to the floor every day.

  • A safe, clean and well equipped working environment
  • Fair wages, fair hours and respect for every individual
  • Ongoing training that builds skill and opportunity
  • Strict compliance with labour law and international standards
  • A culture of teamwork, honesty and work life balance
