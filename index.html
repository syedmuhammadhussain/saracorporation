Introduction

Do Business with One of the Finest Apparel Makers in Pakistan

Founded in 1979, Sara Corporation manages the complete journey of every order, from fabric to finished garment, with core processes under one roof, guided by modern machinery, advanced technology and a skilled workforce.

For buyers around the world, we are a single trusted partner who delivers without compromise. What we promise at the sampling stage is exactly what we deliver at shipment.

1979 — The Beginning

Established as a small CMT apparel unit by our CEO, Mr. Muhammad Azam.

1990s — Independence

Converted into a fully independent manufacturing and export company, growing nationwide.

Today — Modernization

Under Director Mr. Zaid Azam, automated and modernized production delivers high quality, value added garments to international customers.

Volume You Can Rely On

Production Capacity

Each month we produce around one hundred and fifty thousand value added pieces, around 75% knits and 25% woven. Our planning teams match each order to the right line and the right window, while real time monitoring keeps managers aware of progress at every stage.

Because our core processes sit under one roof, we scale output up or down with agility, absorb seasonal peaks and keep our delivery promises.

Prevention, Not Detection

Quality Assurance

Quality is built into every step of our process, not just checked at the end. From fabric inspection to stitching, finishing and packing, our quality team monitors each stage to ensure every product meets customer requirements and international standards.

  • Fabric inspection before cutting, on dedicated machines
  • Panel checking and inline checks at the source
  • Final audits before packing and dispatch
  • Customer audits welcomed, any time
