Who We Are

Four Decades of Making It Properly

Sara Corporation began in 1979 as a small cut-make-trim unit founded by our CEO, Mr. Muhammad Azam. Through the 1990s it grew into a fully independent manufacturing and export company, and today it stands among the established apparel makers of Pakistan.

Under our director, Mr. Zaid Azam, the floor has been modernised and automated, lifting the business into high quality, value added goods that attract customers worldwide.
