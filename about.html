About Sara Corporation

Founded in 1979 by Mr. Muhammad Azam and modernised by our director Mr. Zaid Azam. Four decades of learning how to make a garment properly, and then how to make it at scale.

Our Story

From One Small Unit to an Integrated Export House

Sara Corporation was established in 1979 as a small apparel unit working on a cut-make-trim basis. After years of persistence by our CEO, Mr. Muhammad Azam, the unit was converted into a fully independent manufacturing and export company through the 1990s.

The company grew into a well known manufacturer and exporter, supporting the national economy through export volume and providing thousands of jobs, while following local and international rules and giving our people more than the basics, with a real work life balance.

Later our director, Mr. Zaid Azam, joined the business and brought his textile education and skills to bear. Production was modernised and automated to make the work easier, more efficient and more cost effective, taking the company to the next level of high quality, value added fancy goods that attract international customers.

1979 — The beginning

Established as a small CMT apparel unit in Karachi by Mr. Muhammad Azam.

1990s — Independence

Converted into a fully independent manufacturing and export company.

2000s — Scale

Grew into a nationally recognised exporter employing hundreds of skilled people.

Today — Modernised

Automated cutting, digital patterns and value added finishing under Mr. Zaid Azam.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

01

Our Mission

To provide world class apparel manufacturing through advanced technology, modern automated machinery, a skilled workforce and responsible business practices. We deliver premium quality that meets international standards, with customer satisfaction, timely delivery, competitive pricing and continuous improvement in every part of our operation.

We build long term relationships through consistency, transparency, innovation and reliability — and through sustainable manufacturing, energy efficient systems and investment in green initiatives such as solar.

02

Our Vision

To become a globally recognised and trusted name in apparel by setting benchmarks in quality, sustainability, innovation and operational excellence, expanding our international presence and strengthening partnerships around the world.

We envision technology driven manufacturing, ethical business practice and environmentally responsible operations working together to create lasting value for customers, employees and society.
