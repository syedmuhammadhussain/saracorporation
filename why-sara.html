Why Sara

Six capabilities, one purpose — to remove risk from your production and keep your shipment on schedule.

Our Strengths

Built so Your Order Is Easier, Faster and Safer

01

One Stop Solution

Yarn to finished, packed garment under one roof and one point of responsibility. No hand-offs between vendors, no gaps where accountability disappears.

02

Shorter Lead Times

Core processes stay in house, which protects quality and takes weeks out of the calendar between order confirmation and shipment.

03

Modern Automation

Automatic cutting tables, digital pattern making, auto pocket setters, sherpa bonding and automatic carton packing bring speed with accuracy.

04

Prevention, Not Detection

Fabric inspection, panel checking, inline checks, needle detection and a final AQL audit. Faults are caught at the source instead of at the end.

05

On Time Delivery

Planned capacity, order-to-line matching and real time monitoring keep managers aware of progress and shipments on schedule.

06

Ethical & Compliant

WRAP, C-TPAT, SMETA, GRS, GSV, WCA, ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. We welcome customer audits as a chance to show exactly how we work.
