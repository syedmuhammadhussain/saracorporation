Printed Fabrics

The Printing and Dyeing division serves the internal needs of Sara Corporation and handles the printing and dyeing requirements of outside clients, with the capability to process both woven and knits.

Printing & Dyeing Division

Twelve Colours, Woven or Knit

This is the one centralised division at Sara Corporation. It caters to the internal needs of every other unit and also takes on printing and dyeing work for external clients, which keeps the equipment busy and the operators sharp.

Rotary printing runs up to twelve colours on both knits and woven in any type of print, and flat bed printing covers engineer type prints where the placement has to be exact.
