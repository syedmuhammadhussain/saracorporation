Production

Five modern units, each dedicated to one discipline and independent in all respects. Every unit carries its own machinery, its own quality wing and its own packing line, so an order never waits on a shared bottleneck.

Our Units

Five Units, One Standard

The Printing and Dyeing division is centralised and serves every other unit, as well as external clients. Everything else is dedicated, so capacity in one discipline never eats into another.

Shared Across Every Unit

What Every Unit Has in Common

Computer Aided Planning

Production planning is computerised, so every order is matched to the right line and the right window before it starts.

Centralised Testing Lab

A full centralised testing laboratory serves every unit, covering the essential tests and analysis on fabric and finished goods.

Own QC Wing per Floor

Every stitching floor carries its own QC, inspection, cropping, pressing, folding and packing wing. Nothing travels to find a checker.

High Temperature Cone Dyeing

In house high temperature cone dyeing gives us colour control from the yarn stage rather than the fabric stage.

Modular Chain Basket System

Stitching floors run on a modular chain basket system, which keeps work in progress visible and balanced across the line.

Latest Machinery

Modern machines and current production procedures across every unit, upgraded continuously rather than in occasional jumps.
